Meet kortex

A helper on your computer, not in someone's cloud.

kortex is patina's assistant. Press the Windows key and Space, then type ai and your request.

Runs on your machine

What you ask never leaves it, and it works offline.

Shows before it changes

Every change waits for you: Allow once, Always, or Deny.

Yours to choose

Prefer the cloud? Pick a provider, European ones first. Or switch it off.

What patina stands for

Three promises that come with every install.

Free software by default

Every app it installs is free and open source. Anything else is opt-in.

Your data stays close

No account to create, no telemetry. Your files sync where you choose.

A new OS on a trusted base

Debian 13 underneath. Security updates come straight from Debian.

The look

One look, from the first screen to the last.

Installer, login, apps and terminal share one theme, in light or true-black dark.

  • Windows open in the middle.
  • One quiet colour for every icon.

How it is made

Made in the open.

Every line is public and GPL. patina builds on what Debian and KDE do well, and adds nothing that needs its own security team. Themes and fixes are welcome.
